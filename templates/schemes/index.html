Government Agricultural Schemes & Subsidies

Official details, eligibility criteria, and direct application links for central and state farmer welfare initiatives.

Direct Benefit Transfer

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, GoI
₹6,000 / Year

Income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments of ₹2,000 directly deposited into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of landholding farmer families.

Key Requirements: Land records (Pattadar Passbook / 1-B), Aadhaar card, Active e-KYC status.
Ongoing All Year Official Portal
Risk Insurance

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Crop Loss Financial Protection
Up to 100% Loss

Comprehensive risk insurance against non-preventable natural risks (floods, cyclones, drought, unseasonal rain, pest outbreaks). Farmer premium only 1.5% - 2.0%.

Key Requirements: Notify crop loss within 72 hours of damage via Krishi Loss Report tool or toll-free number.
72-Hour Intimation Window Claim Portal
Energy & Irrigation

PM-KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme

Ministry of New & Renewable Energy
60% Subsidy

Subsidized solar-powered irrigation pumps (3 HP to 10 HP) enabling daytime farming irrigation independent of erratic electric grid supply and zero diesel costs.

Key Requirements: Agricultural land title, borewell/water source with viable water table.
Daytime solar water supply Apply Online
Soil Fertility

Soil Health Card Scheme

Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Free Testing

Free soil nutrient testing (12 parameters: N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) conducted every 2 years with tailored chemical & organic fertilizer doses.

Key Requirements: Submit soil core samples via local Village Agricultural Assistant (VAA) or Rythu Bharosa Kendra.
